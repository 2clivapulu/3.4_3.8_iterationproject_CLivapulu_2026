FAQ'S

Events are divided between the North New Zealand and the South New Zealand Conference. All the events are to gather fellow SDA Christians to target community reach, youth development, spiritual growth and professional networking. Church are known to hold their own events to achieve this goal. Over the year, people can attend the sport tournaments, Pathfinder and Adventurer group and ministries (Women's ministry and Men's ministry) to attend the Conventions and Ministry summits.

All the events are hosted by the North and South New Zealand conferences. The primary sports that are played are: volleyball, soccer, badminton, and touch rugby. The events always incorporate a sabbath worship service or a dedicated Youth Rally.
