ABOUT

The Wellington Samoan Seventh-day Adventist Church is located at 23 Apple Terrace, Ranui, Porirua 5024.

Sabbath service are held on Saturdays. Sabbath school starts at 9:30 AM followed by the main worship service starting around 11:00 AM.

Worship services are often spoken in Samoan. The church worships by a bible-based teaching, healthy living, and faith in the second coming of Jesus Christ.

The church occasionally hosts combines with other churches or a special sabbath. The church also supports community activities, youth programs, and outreach services.

The church has an Adventurer and Pathfinder club, for children aged 5 -16 years old. The purpose of these 2 clubs is for young children to build a relationship with God and learn practical skills.

The church itself has social media platforms and a livestream page. They go LIVE at 11:00 AM when the main service starts.
